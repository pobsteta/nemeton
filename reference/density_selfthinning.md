# Species self-thinning relationships (Charru et al. 2012)

Lookup table of \\\ln(N\_{max}) = a + b \ln(D_g) + c \ln(D_g)^2\\
coefficients estimated from French National Forest Inventory data for 11
temperate and Mediterranean tree species, published in:

## Details

Charru, M., Seynave, I., Morneau, F., Rivoire, M. & Bontemps, J.-D.
(2012). *Significant differences and curvilinearity in the self-thinning
relationships of 11 temperate tree species assessed from forest
inventory data.* Annals of Forest Science 69(2), 195-205. (HAL:
hal-00930719).

When the quadratic term was significant in the paper (7 species), the
curvilinear model (Table 5) is used. Otherwise the linear self-thinning
line (Table 2) is retained.
